Timers

Professional timing relays and controllers for industrial automation. Features on-delay, off-delay, star-delta, staircase, and multi-function timers with adjustable time ranges from 0.1 seconds to 100 hours.

Delay Timer

DTR-30 – On/Off Delay Timer (0.1s-30s)

The DTR-30 On/Off Delay Timer operates at 230V AC or 24V AC/DC, with 50/60 Hz frequency, < 6 VA power, and provides adjustable on-delay or off-delay timing from 0.1 to 30 seconds.
Delay Timer

DTR-60 – On/Off Delay Timer (0.1s-60s)

The DTR-60 On/Off Delay Timer operates at 230V AC or 24V AC/DC, with 50/60 Hz frequency, < 6 VA power, and provides adjustable on-delay or off-delay timing from 0.1 to 60 seconds.
Delay Timer

DTR-03M – On/Off Delay Timer (0.1s-3min)

The DTR-03M On/Off Delay Timer operates at 230V AC or 24V AC/DC, with 50/60 Hz frequency, < 6 VA power, and provides adjustable on-delay or off-delay timing from 0.1 seconds to 3 minutes.
Star-delta

DTR-SD – Star-Delta Timer for Motor Starting

The DTR-SD Star-Delta Timer operates at 180–230V AC, 50/60 Hz, with < 6 VA power, and provides star-delta timing for motor starting, with star delay from 0.1 to 30 seconds and waiting time from 1 ms to 500 ms.
Staircase

DTR-SC – Staircase Timer for Lighting Control

The DTR-SC Staircase Timer operates at 180–230V AC, 50/60 Hz, with < 6 VA power, and provides adjustable timing from 1 second to 20 minutes for lighting control applications.
Timer

FTR-07 – Right-Left Timer (0.1s-100min)

The FTR-07 Right-Left Alternating Timer operates at 12–230V AC/DC, 50/60 Hz, with < 6 VA power, and provides adjustable alternating timing from 0.1 seconds to 100 minutes.

Delay Timer

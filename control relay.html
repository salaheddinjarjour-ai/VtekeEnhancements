Control Relays

Advanced control relays for automated system control including photocell relays, liquid level control relays, and pump alternation controllers. Reliable automation solutions for industrial and commercial applications.

PCR-03 – Photocell Relay (Fixed Lux Control)

The PCR-03 Photocell Relay (Fixed Lux Control) operates at 180–230V AC, 50/60 Hz, < 6 VA, and provides fixed light-level (lux) control using a photoresistor sensor, with a response time of less than 1 second.
PHOTOCELL

PCR-04 – Photocell Relay (Adjustable Lux Control)

The PCR-04 Photocell Relay (Adjustable Lux Control) operates at 180–230V AC, 50/60 Hz, < 6 VA, and provides adjustable light-level (lux) control using a photoresistor sensor, with a response time of less than 1 second.
LIQUID LEVEL

LLR-05 – Liquid Level Control Relay (Single Tank)

The LLR-05 Liquid Level Control Relay (Single Tank) operates at 85–230V AC/DC, 50/60 Hz, < 6 VA, and provides single-tank liquid level control using conductive electrodes with a 3-electrode sensing system.
LIQUID LEVEL

LLR-05U – Liquid Level Control Relay with Adjustable Time Delay

The LLR-05U Liquid Level Control Relay with Adjustable Time Delay operates at 85–230V AC/DC, 50/60 Hz, < 6 VA, and provides single-tank liquid level control using conductive electrodes, with an adjustable time delay of 0.5–10 s.
DUAL TANK

LLR-06 – Dual Tank Liquid Level Control Relay

The LLR-06 Dual Tank Liquid Level Control Relay operates at 85–230V AC/DC, 50/60 Hz, < 6 VA, and provides dual-tank liquid level control using conductive electrodes for two tanks.
2-PUMP

HPR-02M – 2-Pump Alternation Relay (Hydrophore Controller)

The HPR-02M 2-Pump Alternation Relay (Hydrophore Controller) operates at 150–250V AC, 50/60 Hz, < 6 VA, and provides automatic alternation (rotation) control for two pumps.
3-PUMP

HPR-03M – 3-Pump Alternation Relay (Hydrophore Controller)

The HPR-02M 2-Pump Alternation Relay (Hydrophore Controller) operates at 150–250V AC, 50/60 Hz, < 6 VA, and provides automatic alternation (rotation) control for two pumps.

Who We Are

VTEKE is a certified manufacturer of industrial low-voltage electrical equipment, delivering precision-engineered solutions to more than 35 countries worldwide. Specializing in current transformers (CTs), electronic protection relays, digital panel meters, and energy analyzers, VTEKE serves electrical panel builders, distributors, and industrial automation professionals who require accuracy, reliability, and long-term performance. Our production is certified under ISO 9001:2015 and all products carry the CE mark, with independent TÜV testing to ensure compliance, safety, and consistent quality. We also hold TSE certification, comply with EAC requirements, and are registered under SABER/SASO for the Saudi market. Driven by continuous research and development, VTEKE focuses on improving measurement precision, durability, and overall product performance, supporting safe operation, energy efficiency, and stable electrical systems in modern industrial installations.
